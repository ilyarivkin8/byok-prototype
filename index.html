    FAQ

    Most-asked, answered right here.

    What is BlazePod?

    BlazePod is a training platform built around light-up Pods that push your strength, sharpen your focus, and increase your speed. Control the Pods through an app loaded with hundreds of free drills designed by pro trainers and used by elite sports clubs and athletes around the world.

    Can I use them outside?

    Yes. BlazePod Pods are durable, weatherproof, and built for indoor and outdoor use — at home, in the yard, on the pitch, in the court, anywhere you train.

    Is the app free?

    Yes. The BlazePod app comes free with hundreds of pro drills for sports and fitness. A premium Pro subscription is available for advanced features like player management and analytics — you can add it later, anytime.

    Do you ship worldwide?

    Yes. We ship worldwide. Shipping costs depend on kit size and location. You'll see the final price, including shipping, at checkout.

    What if I change my mind?

    You have 14 days from delivery to request a full refund — no questions asked. (Shipping and taxes are not refundable.)
